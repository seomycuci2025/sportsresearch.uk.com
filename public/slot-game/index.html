Understanding RTP, Volatility & Winning Potential in Slot Malaysia

As a slot game player, RTP, volatility, and winning potential are key elements that you must understand before choosing your ideal slot game. Learn about them below:

What is RTP (Return to Player)?

This refers to the percentage of overall bets that you are likely to get back over time. For instance, if you choose a slot title with 96% RTP, it means you have a very high chance of landing winnings with your bet compared to slot titles with lower RTP.

Understanding Volatility (Variance)

This refers to how risky a game can be. A slot title with high volatility means rare chances of winning but massive potential for major winnings. In contrast, low volatility means frequent small winnings for players.
